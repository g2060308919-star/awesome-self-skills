## Implemented benchmark boundary

- `benchmark/gates.mjs` validates candidate bindings, exact system/domain/risk
  topology, all nine metrics and confidence intervals, and categorical failure
  priority without averaging away a weak slice.
- `benchmark/score.mjs` loads only retained local evidence, derives eligibility
  from acquisition ledgers rather than capture self-report, binds bytes to one
  committed regular-blob tree, and carries the loader-owned candidate binding
  through `scoreBenchmark` to the gate.
- `benchmark/manifest.schema.json` requires the closed candidate-binding policy
  and both acquisition-ledger references.
- `reviewer-acquisition.json` binds two synthetic reviewers, one synthetic
  adjudicator, their label records, and adjudication. These identities are
  categorically ineligible for release.
- `capture-acquisition.json` binds twelve synthetic sessions: four systems ×
  three repeats for one retained PRD. Each session binds source, task, system,
  raw output, extraction, independence assertion, and attestation digest.
- The protocol states the governance limit honestly: repository strings and
  hashes make evidence auditable but cannot themselves prove that a human review
  or external execution actually occurred.
